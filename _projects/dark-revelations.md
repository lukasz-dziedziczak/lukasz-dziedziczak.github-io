## Overview

Dark Revelations is a third-person horror game set during a viral outbreak. Players explore a quarantined facility, uncover its story, and survive encounters using a tactical dismemberment system.

I developed the project solo over three months for SAE’s Interactive Digital Narratives unit.

## Design focus

I designed the levels, combat, and story encounters, using environmental detail to carry much of the narrative.

The project also included motion capture for an in-game news broadcast.
