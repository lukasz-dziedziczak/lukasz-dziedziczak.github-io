## Overview

Exfil Void is a twin-stick extraction shooter released on Steam in March 2026. Players explore procedurally generated stations, collect Tech Cores, assemble a build, and decide when to extract.

I designed, programmed, tested, and published the game independently through Emberline Studios.

## Selected systems

- Modular, data-driven procedural station generation
- Combat, AI, navigation, persistence, and progression
- Stackable perks and weapon unlocks
- Steam achievements, Cloud support, and store delivery
