## Overview

Space Servo is a management game about running a remote space petrol station. Players manage resources, refuel ships, upgrade facilities, and serve alien customers.

It was developed over three months as a team project for a Commercial Studio class.

## My contribution

I worked across production, programming, and design, focusing on gameplay implementation, economy balancing, and project management.
