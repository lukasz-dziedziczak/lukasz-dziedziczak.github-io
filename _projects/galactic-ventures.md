## Overview

Galactic Ventures is a mobile asteroid-mining game I developed independently in my spare time and released in open beta on Google Play.

Players extract resources, trade across stations, explore a procedurally generated galaxy, and improve their ships.

## Selected systems

- A trading economy with fluctuating prices
- Procedural star systems and encounters
- Crafting and modular spacecraft upgrades
- Derelicts, discoveries, and narrative logs
