## Overview

I created this VR training module during a work experience placement at SA Power Networks. It guides users through an insulator change on a Stobie pole.

Using supplied assets, I developed the experience in Unreal Engine for Meta Quest 2, with a focus on clear interaction and procedural accuracy.
